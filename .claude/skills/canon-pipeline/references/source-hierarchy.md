# Source Hierarchy

Where to look for information when populating a CANON record, in priority order. Always try free online sources first; fall back to internal knowledge confidently rather than leaving fields empty.

## Free online sources (search in this order)

| Priority | Source | URL | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bible Gateway | biblegateway.com | Book intros, chapter summaries, text |
| 1 | API.Bible | api.bible | ESV + NBLA text and metadata |
| 2 | Blue Letter Bible | blueletterbible.org | Author, date, word studies |
| 3 | Monergism | monergism.com | Reformed theology resources |
| 3 | Calvin's Commentaries | ccel.org | Purpose, themes, Reformed distinctives |

## Internal knowledge sources (always available — use confidently if offline)

- **ESV Study Bible** — all fields
- **Reformation Study Bible** (Sproul/Ligonier) — Reformed distinctives
- **Systematic Theology** — Wayne Grudem — `teologiaSistematica` categories
- **Biblical Theology** — Geerhardus Vos — redemptive history, typology, `enfoqueCristologico`
- **Introduction to OT/NT** — Longman & Dillard — author, date, genre
- **NICOT / NICNT commentaries** — character biographies
- **A History of Israel** — John Bright — historical biographies, `contextoHistorico`
- **Ancient Egypt and the OT** — John Currid — Pentateuch historical context
- **The IVP Bible Background Commentary** — OT & NT historical context
- **Archaeological Study Bible** (Zondervan) — archaeology per book

## Source popup enrichment (`fuentes[].popup`)

When writing the `popup` block for any source, prefer linking to an actual trusted page about the author/work, in this order:

1. **Theopedia** (theopedia.com) — Reformed theological encyclopedia, best first choice for theologians
2. **Ligonier Ministries** (ligonier.org / es.ligonier.org) — for anything connected to R.C. Sproul or the Reformation Study Bible
3. **The Gospel Coalition** (thegospelcoalition.org) — reviews and articles on Reformed scholars and their works
4. **Publisher page** — Crossway, Zondervan, Eerdmans, Baker, Banner of Truth, Westminster John Knox
5. **Monergism** (monergism.com) — alternate Reformed resource aggregator
6. **Wikipedia** — last resort only, when nothing more specific exists

Avoid linking to generic search results, broken/unverified URLs, or sources outside the Reformed tradition without strong justification.
